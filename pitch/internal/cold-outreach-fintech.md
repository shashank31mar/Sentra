# Cold Outreach Template — Fintech / Payments AI & Risk Leaders (Tier 1)

**For:** [prospect-list.md](prospect-list.md) Tier 1 (money-moving agent companies)
**Motion:** Design partner (NOT investor) — **never attach the investor one-pager.**
**Channel:** LinkedIn connect/InMail first → move to email when they engage.

> Founder-led prospecting style: plain subject, sign as **Founder** (not CEO), no Amazon mention, soft ask, link/material only *after* they bite. Keep it short — these people get pitched daily.

---

## A. LinkedIn connection note (≤300 chars — the first touch)

> Hi [First] — building AegionAI Guard: a runtime control layer that *enforces* what AI agents are allowed to do when they move money (refunds, payouts, transfers), not just detect after. Given [Company]'s agent work, would value 15 min of your read. Worth a quick exchange?

## B. InMail / first DM (if no connection, slightly longer)

**Subject:** AI agents moving money at [Company] — runtime guardrails

> Hi [First],
>
> I'm the founder of AegionAI Guard. As agents start taking real money actions — refunds, transfers, card issuance — the gap I keep hearing about is *enforcement*: detection tools warn after the fact, but nothing blocks an unsafe action at runtime or rolls it back.
>
> We're building that layer (real-time policy enforcement + rollback for agent actions, cross-cloud, deployable in your VPC). We're picking a small number of design partners in payments to build with — your name came up given [specific: e.g. "Ramp's applied-AI work" / "Radar / Payments Intelligence"].
>
> Not selling anything — looking for one or two sharp partners to shape this. Worth 20 minutes to compare notes?
>
> — [Name], Founder, AegionAI Guard

## D. The soft ask (what you actually want)
A 20-min call to (1) hear how they think about agent-action risk, (2) show the two-cloud enforcement demo, (3) see if a lightweight design-partner pilot makes sense. No commitment framing.

## E. Follow-up cadence (don't over-chase)
- **T+0:** connect note / InMail (A or B).
- **T+4–5 days:** if accepted but silent, one short nudge: *"Still keen to get your read — even a 'not now' helps me focus. Happy to send a 1-pager if useful."*
- **T+10–12 days:** final value-add touch (share a relevant incident/insight, e.g. the 340%-YoY prompt-injection stat or a 2026 agent incident), then stop. Move on; revisit when you have a marquee logo.

## F. Guardrails (per outreach-style memory + tracker rules)
- Sign **Founder**, not CEO. No Amazon reference in writing.
- Link / one-pager / demo **after** they show interest, not in the first message.
- **Design-partner one-pager only** — the *investor* one-pager never goes to this list.
- Log every send in [outreach-tracker.md](outreach-tracker.md) with date + state.
