Respected Sir / Madam,

We are grateful for the opportunity to meet and to contribute to the Government of Assam's vision for an AI-first administration and for what your Government has aptly called Assam's Techade. The foundations are already in place — the digital public infrastructure built over the past four years, from the Sewa Setu platform now delivering hundreds of citizen services to the consolidation of the State's systems within the Assam State Data Centre; the recent Steering Committee on AI Adoption and the proposed State-wide AI architecture; the Assam State Data Policy and the new Centre for Data Management; and the early use of agentic AI to help officials process citizen-service applications, with a human official kept firmly in the loop. As AI systems increasingly act on the Government's behalf, the security of what those systems are permitted to do becomes as important as the intelligence itself. AegionAI is being built precisely for this challenge, and we would be honoured to support Assam's programmes in digital and defence security.

About AegionAI

AegionAI is a security company building a neutral control layer that governs the actions of AI agents in real time — evaluating what an action does (its parameters, the identity behind it, and the sequence of steps) and stopping a harmful action the instant it occurs, rather than merely inspecting the text of a prompt. In short, we make autonomous AI safe to deploy in high-stakes environments.

  • Action-level protection: blocks harmful or unauthorised AI actions before they execute, with a fail-secure, deny-by-default design.
  • Vendor-neutral: one governance layer across major cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and on-premises systems — no lock-in to a single provider.
  • Built for sovereignty: deployment options span cloud, private-cloud (in-VPC), and fully on-premises / air-gapped installations, so sensitive data and models can remain entirely within the State's own boundary.
  • Tamper-evident audit: an append-only record of every AI decision, designed as an accountable system-of-record for oversight and compliance.

Our experience

Our founding team brings over 13 years of experience designing and scaling enterprise-grade technology platforms — including services that have grown into multi-billion-dollar (US $6 billion+) global businesses at one of the world's largest technology companies. We have translated that experience in building secure, reliable systems at massive scale into a working AI-security platform, and we bring deep expertise in cloud security, large-scale systems, and applied machine learning.

Relevance to Assam's digital & defence security priorities

As the State deploys AI across citizen services, governance, and security, AegionAI can serve as the protective layer that keeps those AI systems safe, accountable, and sovereign:

  • Secure the AI agents the State adopts so they cannot be manipulated, misused, or exceed their authorised scope.
  • Keep data and AI models within India / within Assam through on-premises and air-gapped deployment — a natural fit for the Assam State Data Centre and the new Centre for Data Management, where the State's systems and citizen data are increasingly consolidated.
  • Provide a verifiable, tamper-evident audit trail for oversight, investigation, and the independent audits and impact assessments now expected under India's data-protection framework.
  • Remain independent of any single technology vendor, protecting the State's long-term flexibility and negotiating position across cloud and on-premises systems.

Initiatives we would like to explore with the State

Rather than a new idea to be adopted, each of the following strengthens a programme the State has already committed to. We offer them as starting points for discussion:

  1. Enforcing the human-in-the-loop on agentic Sewa Setu. The State has rightly made human oversight mandatory for its agentic-AI use. AegionAI can make that rule enforceable in the system itself — checking every action an AI agent attempts (its parameters, the identity behind it, and the sequence of steps), blocking an unauthorised or harmful action before it executes, and recording each decision in a tamper-evident log for accountability.
  2. A data-sovereignty control layer for the State data repository. As the Centre for Data Management builds a unified, State-wide data repository for analytics and AI, AegionAI can govern what AI systems are permitted to do against that data — deployed inside the State's own environment (in-VPC or fully air-gapped) so data and enforcement never leave Assam's boundary, with audit evidence ready for compliance.
  3. A sovereign AI-security deployment in the State's data-centre infrastructure. Our fully self-contained (“nothing leaves”) deployment can run inside Assam's sovereign data-centre environment, so protection for AI workloads that handle sensitive citizen data stays entirely within the State's control.
  4. A local partnership for indigenous, trusted AI security. We would welcome co-developing and testing these capabilities with Assam's academic and research institutions — building local capacity and an India-origin approach to safe and trusted AI.

Securing borders and defence with AI

Assam's position on India's international frontier, and your Government's rights-based, technology-enabled approach to policing, make security a priority of a different order. As these functions increasingly use AI — to analyse surveillance and sensor feeds, support intelligence and situational-awareness decisions, and operate autonomous systems — the AI itself becomes a target and a point of failure. AegionAI does not build the surveillance, forensic, or command systems themselves, nor the sensors or cameras they rely on; we secure the AI that runs on top of them, so it can be trusted in the field:

  • Non-manipulable AI: we detect and block attempts to deceive or hijack an AI system — through manipulated inputs, poisoned data, or injected instructions — before it can act on them, so an adversary cannot turn the State's own AI against it.
  • Actions stay within authorised limits: an AI agent supporting a security or defence decision is held to exactly what it is permitted to do; any action beyond its mandate is stopped before it executes, keeping a human in command of consequential decisions.
  • Fully sovereign and offline-capable: our air-gapped deployment runs entirely within secured premises with no external connectivity — nothing leaves the network — which is essential for classified and frontier environments.
  • Tamper-evident record: every AI-assisted action is logged in an append-only, tamper-evident trail, providing accountability and a clear chain of custody for review and investigation.
